How the forecast is built

The label is 1 when a household bought banana product 1082185 with a positive quantity in the 28 days after a reference day. Features use only days up to the reference day. Training, validation and test periods are separated in time with a 28-day gap, so no future days leak into training.

Read the list with these limits in mind

  • The forecast covers the 28 days after the last day in the data. That period has no data, so it cannot be checked. The backtest is the evidence.
  • Hit rates belong to deciles. A household in the top decile is in a group that bought bananas often, which is not the same as a personal probability.
  • The model relies heavily on each household's banana history, and the same households appear in training and testing. It says little about households with no history.
  • The test period was viewed once before the purchase definition changed to require a positive quantity. Model choices were made on validation and the rolling folds.
  • The data covers about two years of one retailer. It describes patterns in that period, not causes and not today's market.
